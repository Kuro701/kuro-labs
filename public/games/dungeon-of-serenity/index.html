How to play

A/D or Left/Right to move. Stand under a ladder and hold S/Down to descend to the next floor, or W/Up to climb back.

The floor-hole art (the circular pit) marks the same connection from the floor above — nothing drops you through it yet, that's the floor-hole kill-threshold event, still to build. The room at floor 5's middle slot is the final gate (the closed wheel door); the blown-open tunnel art already exists (assets/floor5_room2_open.jpg) but isn't wired to any trigger yet either — both are next-system work, not part of this pass.
