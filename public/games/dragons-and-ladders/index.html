      How to play
      • Take turns rolling the die and moving that many squares. Reach square 100 first to win.
      • Land on the foot of a ladder and you climb to its top.
      • Land on a dragon's head and it carries you down to its tail.
      • You need the exact number to land on 100. Roll too high and you stay put.
      • Online: one player creates a room and shares the 5-character code (or the invite link). Friends join with it, the host can add bots and starts the game. The host can also remove a player or close the room.
      • Logged in on kurolabs.net? Wins in online rooms with two or more people are saved to your account and can appear on the leaderboard. You can hide yourself from it in your account settings.
