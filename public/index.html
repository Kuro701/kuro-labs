Jiří, or Kuro to most people. I build things that probably shouldn't exist and then make them work anyway: software that runs on my own machine, assistants that remember, props with unwholesome contents, and games my friends can't stop arguing over.

Most of it starts as a stupid idea at two in the morning. Some of it ends up here. If you want something built, the four things below are what I actually do — and I'd rather tell you a job is out of my depth than take your money and find out halfway.

What I do

Four kinds of work

I

Custom software & AI assistants

Tools built for one person's actual workflow instead of everyone's average one. Including personal AI assistants that run locally, keep their own memory, and answer to you rather than a subscription.

II

3D models & VR assets

Modelled in Blender, optimised properly. Props, decor and world pieces for VRChat on PC and Quest, or standalone models for whatever you had in mind.

III

Web design

Small, fast, unique sites. No template you've seen on forty other pages, no framework weighing four megabytes to show a paragraph of text.

IV

Custom games

Browser multiplayer that works on a phone in a Discord call. Private rooms, no accounts, no install — send a code and play.
