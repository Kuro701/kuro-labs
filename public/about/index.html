Kuro Labs

Helping creators
get seen.

Kuro Labs helps small digital creators get their work out into the world. We handle product listings, storefronts and promotion, so creators can spend more time making things.

It started with one person building things that probably shouldn’t exist and then making them work anyway: VRChat props with unwholesome contents, software that runs on his own machine, assistants that remember, and games friends can’t stop arguing over.

Kuro still builds all of that. Now he does it alongside creators like Galvandus, whose assets go through the same shop, with the listings, marketing and posting taken off their hands. Most of it still starts as a stupid idea at two in the morning. Some of it ends up here.

If you want something built, we’d rather tell you a job is out of our depth than take your money and find out halfway.
