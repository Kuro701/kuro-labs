Kuro Labs

A small lab.
A few makers.

Software, VRChat assets and games, built by Kuro and a growing team of creators.

Kuro Labs started as one person building things that probably shouldn’t exist and then making them work anyway: VRChat props with unwholesome contents, software that runs on his own machine, assistants that remember, and games friends can’t stop arguing over.

Now it’s a small team. Kuro and Galvandus both make VRChat assets for the shop, and Kuro also builds the software, games and this site. Most of it still starts as a stupid idea at two in the morning. Some of it ends up here.

If you want something built, we’d rather tell you a job is out of our depth than take your money and find out halfway.
