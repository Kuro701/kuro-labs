What do I receive?

Software is delivered as a repository with setup documentation. 3D delivery is agreed for the project, including Unity packages, FBX and textures where appropriate.

Can you build for Quest?

Platform support is agreed per asset. Quest versions need separate optimisation. Crescent Rose in the shop is PCVR only.

What about local AI?

Share your GPU and the intended workload early so the scope can match the hardware.
