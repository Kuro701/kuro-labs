About this asset

Harbinger is a transforming VRChat weapon with five forms: Sword, Extended Sword, Gun, Scythe and Packed. Featuring animated transformations, moving cogwheels, a katana-style wrapped handle and an extending red scythe edge. Includes an easy avatar installer and installation guide, custom menu icons with separate transformation controls, hand and back placement with adjustable fitting and overall size, Show/Hide, World Lock and Flip Grip, and left-hand click-to-reposition along the deployed scythe handle. Gun mode includes the transformation; firing effects are not included. Requires your own humanoid avatar, VRChat Avatars SDK, VRCFury and Poiyomi Toon 9.3 (not included). Quest headsets can use it through a PC connection; standalone Quest/Android is not supported. Personal avatar use only; modification for your own avatars is allowed. No redistribution, sharing, resale or inclusion in avatars offered for sale.
