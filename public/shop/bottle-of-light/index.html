About this asset

Bottle of Light v1.14 by Galvandus. Carry a little light with you in PC VRChat. Adjust light intensity and sweep the colour from white through yellow to red from your avatar menu. The light works with the stopper open or closed. Choose from four configurable attachment positions with independent sizing, and customize the cork, metal, glass and effects tints in Unity. Includes the Unity package, an installation guide and technical stats. Requires VRChat SDK Avatars, VRCFury and Poiyomi Toon (not included). Final VRChat performance rank depends on your complete avatar, so budget using the higher figures listed. Use on your own private and public VRChat avatars is allowed; resale of the asset or its parts is prohibited.
