Pick the wordmark

Same size, same glow, same colour — only the typeface changes. Whichever you pick becomes one line in assets/style.css (--display). Look at them at arm's length, not up close: the question is which one you'd recognise as a logo in a Discord embed at thumbnail size.

The tension worth naming
Options 2 and 5 read as metal band. That's great for the games and the cursed prop shop — and it's working against you on the page where someone decides whether to pay you to build their software. 1, 3 and 4 stay gothic without costing you that. Options 3 and 4 are the ones that still read at 32px in a browser tab.
